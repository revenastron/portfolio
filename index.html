About me

My Trajectory

2026 – Present

A New Horizon

Currently operating as a freelance developer while steering my ship firmly toward academia. As I prepare for the UGC NET, my ultimate mission is to transition into full-time teaching. My goal is to bring real-world industry experience, digital simulators, and modern problem-solving directly into the classroom to guide the next generation of learners.

The Convergence

A pivotal phase where my technical trajectory aligned with a core part of my character: the natural urge to share what I know and explain complex ideas to others.
